01 / A LITTLE ABOUT MEMORE THAN ONE LENS

I like questions
that don’t fit in
one subject.

I’m a high school sophomore in the IB Middle Years Programme (MYP) at NOVA International School in Skopje, North Macedonia. An international education is teaching me to look at the world through more than one lens.

I love the precision of mathematics and statistics, but also the stories a model can’t quite capture. How do we make decisions? What makes a community thrive? And what do we carry with us from the cultures we grow up in?

That curiosity follows me into piano, poetry, creative writing, and translation, too. For me, the interesting part is finding the connections.
